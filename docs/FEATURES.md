# Implemented workflows and limitations

| Capability | Current behavior | Remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submission | Download readiness report, official templates and reviewed Truro form draft; link to official portals with guided steps | Direct submission only after an officially supported authorized integration is established |
| Required-form discovery | Municipality-scoped verified catalog with original PDFs and clear distinctions between application, requirements and guidance; deterministic conditional HRM checklist | Additional verified jurisdiction/permit rule packs; broader AI-assisted scope discovery |
| Form assistance | Genuine model-assisted document extraction when Workers AI is enabled; grounded field suggestions, explicit reviewed apply and provenance; reviewed official Truro address/description autofill | More forms and reviewed field mappings; original scans/OCR |
| Auditing | Deterministic applicability/evidence evaluation, missing/conflicting/unknown findings, official citations, corrections and activity history | Qualified professional validation; full building-code analysis outside prototype scope |
| Renewal | User-recorded permit lifecycle, draft preparation, unknown eligibility exposed, explicit human-review approval for manual handoff; changes to permit facts invalidate approval | Verified eligibility rules and authorized submission integrations |
| Expiration | User-provided issue/expiry dates and expiry source, configurable in-app reminders, real ICS calendar export | Background email/push and synchronized server records |
| Discrepancy detection | Grounded document address, project name/scope, same-role identities and contractor comparisons; reviewed correction | Wider structured fields and drawing interpretation |

Records and extracted text are stored in the current browser. Uploaded originals are not retained. No authentication or team synchronization is implemented. A different browser has separate projects.

Only Halifax commercial renovations receive automated regulatory applicability assessment. Cape Breton, Truro, Kentville, Bridgewater and other Nova Scotia locations use manual tracking and official-resource references. A downloaded form does not establish its applicability or submission completeness. Some catalog entries describe development or energy applications, and are explicitly distinguished from general building permits.

AI output never creates regulatory rules. Strict schemas and grounded evidence checks validate extraction; errors/timeouts fall back to labelled deterministic methods. Scans or complex drawings may lack extractable text. Extracted text does not verify engineering adequacy.

Synthetic seeded data and demo documents contain no real customer information. No permit is issued, signed or submitted by this prototype. Readiness counts describe supporting evidence, not legal compliance or approval.

Official resource catalog: 28 entries and 18 distinct original PDF templates, including seven Government of Nova Scotia PDFs. Search by purpose and jurisdiction. Provincial forms are original downloads with professional/septic/fuel/appeal scope caveats; no provincial autofill or new automated applicability rules. Original PDF hashes and readability are separately tested; editing support is not inferred from field counts.
