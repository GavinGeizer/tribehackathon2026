# PermitPath — Pilot Metrics (proposal, no results)

Status: no pilots run. No customer data, no measured outcomes. Figures below are *what to measure*, not claims.

## Pilot shape (proposal)

- Customer pilots with repeat professional applicants; observation-only, synthetic or applicant-owned test packages.
- Each pilot: run readiness check, record findings, applicant files independently. We never file.
- Duration proposal: 2–4 weeks or 2 packages per participant, whichever comes first.
- Validate: frequency of preparation burden, time cost, willingness to pay. No interviews conducted yet.

## Metrics to collect

| # | Metric | Definition | Target (proposal, unvalidated) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Avoidable follow-ups flagged (proxy) | Missing/conflicting items flagged before filing that participant confirms would have gone in | Track count; no target claimed |
| 2 | Missing-item catch rate | Flagged missing applicable items / total applicable missing found incl. post-hoc | Report only |
| 3 | False-flag rate | Flags participant disputes with reason / total flags | Report; investigate high rates |
| 4 | Time to readiness report | Document intake to exported report (minutes) | Report median |
| 5 | Inconsistency catches | Mismatched-address or conflicting-evidence flags confirmed | Count |
| 6 | Human-review queue depth | Items needing human review / undetermined per package | Report; should be non-zero (honest unknowns) |
| 7 | Usability | Participant completes preparation path unaided (yes/no + notes) | Record blockers |

## Non-goals for pilot

No compliance-rate claims, no turnaround-time guarantees, no pricing validation beyond willingness-to-discuss. Professional-evidence items always end in human review regardless of metrics.

## Pricing discovery (unvalidated hypothesis)

Ask only: per-project vs subscription preference; who approves spend; how much preparation burden matters. Record answers; do not set prices from anecdotes. No downtime or financial claims.
