## Problem (prescribed text, 62 words)

Preparing a Halifax commercial renovation application requires matching project-specific supporting documents to municipal instructions. Contractors must decide which requirements apply, collect evidence, and check consistency before submission. Missing or conflicting information can create avoidable follow-up and rework. PermitPath tests whether a source-backed readiness workspace can reduce that preparation burden. We will validate frequency, time cost, and willingness to pay through customer pilots.

## Buyer hypothesis (unvalidated — hypothesis only)

Repeat professional applicants (hypothesis only, unvalidated): contractors or designers who repeatedly assemble Halifax commercial permit packages. ## What PermitPath is / is not (prototype scope)

- IS (planned): browser-local project workspace; document upload + text extraction; deterministic conditional rules citing official Halifax source rows; evidence quotes with page numbers; address-mismatch flag; resolve + export report.
- Deterministic rules first; optional AI extraction adapter only if configured, with deterministic fallback explicitly labeled.
- IS NOT: no municipal integration, no electronic submission to HRM, no compliance guarantee, no engineered-drawing verification, no fee estimate.
