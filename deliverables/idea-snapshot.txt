Team: Space Jam
Project: PermitPath
Sector: Small Businesses / Construction Technology

Preparing a Halifax commercial renovation application requires matching project-specific supporting documents to municipal instructions. Contractors must determine which requirements apply, gather evidence, and check consistency before submission. Missing or conflicting information can create avoidable follow-up and rework. PermitPath tests whether a source-backed readiness workspace can reduce this preparation burden: trace requirements to official sources, connect documents to evidence, and make unresolved questions visible. Customer pilots will validate the frequency of these problems, preparation time, and willingness to pay.
